PRIVACY POLICY

Student Report Sheet Generator

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Student Report Sheet Generator ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. The Service is operated by Ibrahim Abdullahi.

Information We Collect

Account information: your email address and password, used to sign in via our authentication provider (Supabase).

School information: your school's name, address, and logo, which you choose to enter so it can appear on generated report sheets.

Student data you upload: names, class, and scores contained in the Excel file you upload, used only to generate report sheets for the students you enter.

Subscription & usage records: your chosen plan, payment reference, and the number of reports you have generated, used to enforce your plan's allowance and provide support if you contact us.

Payment information: payments are handled directly by Paystack. We do not receive or store your card number or banking details - Paystack shares only a payment reference and status with us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign back in.
  • To generate the report sheets you request, and to let you re-display a previously generated report without being charged again.
  • To track and enforce the report allowance included in your subscription plan.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To send account-related messages, such as password resets or subscription confirmations.

We do not sell your data, your school's data, or your students' data to anyone, and we do not use student data for advertising.

Where Your Data Is Stored

User's Account, subscription details,is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase. Some information (such as the record of which reports you have already generated) is also kept in your browser's local storage on the device you use, so the Service works smoothly between visits.

Sharing With Third Parties

We share only what is necessary to run the Service:

  • Supabase - hosts our database and handles account sign-in.
  • Paystack - processes subscription payments.

We do not share student data with any other third party, and we do not use it to train external services.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data Retention

We keep your account and subscription records for as long as your account is active, so your report history and allowance stay accurate. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your account and uploaded student data, except where we are required to keep payment records for accounting or legal purposes.

Schools Manager · Last updated October 19, 2026

Schools Manager is a tool that schools use to manage class lists, scores, attendance and student report cards. This page explains what information the app handles and, in particular, how its Google Drive backup feature works.

Information we handle

When a school owner or staff member uses the app, the school's records are saved in the school's own account. These include class lists, student names and admission numbers, scores, attendance, passport photos and school profile details. This information is used only to run the app for that school. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Google Drive backup

The app has an optional feature that lets a school owner back up completed academic sessions to their own Google Drive, and restore them later. If you use it, you are asked to sign in with Google and to allow the following permission:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file).

With this permission the app can only see the files and folder that it created itself, a folder called "Schools Manager Backups". It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

What happens to your Google data

  • Backup files are saved to your Google Drive, using your own storage space.
  • Backups are sent directly from your browser to Google Drive. We do not receive a copy, and we cannot read your backup files.
  • Your Google access token is kept in your browser's memory while the page is open and is discarded when you close or refresh the page. We do not store it on our servers.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to anyone.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can use backup

Only the school owner can back up or restore data. Teacher and staff accounts do not have access to this feature.

Student information

Student records belong to the school. The school is responsible for having the right to hold and use this information, and for deciding who in the school can access it.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any backup file at any time from your own Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access. To ask for school records to be deleted from the app, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
